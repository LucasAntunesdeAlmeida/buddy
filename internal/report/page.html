How Buddy thinks

Every 30 seconds Buddy looks at what you're doing and gives each thing he could do a score: how much reward he expects over the next few minutes. Then he does the best one, which is usually nothing.

  1. He looks. Which kind of app you're in, how busy your keyboard and mouse are, how long you've been at it, the time, and how annoyed he thinks you are.
  2. He scores. His brain turns that into a score for each action. A click from you is worth +1, being sent away −2, and every attempt costs a little.
  3. He learns. Every reaction goes into his diary, a table that keeps exact tallies. Every couple of hours his neural network studies the diary, so next time he guesses better.
